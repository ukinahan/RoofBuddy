Roof Inspector · Companion Web Portal

Assistant & Inspector Guide

How to access, navigate, and use admin.roofinspector.app alongside the iPhone app.
Version 1.1 · April 2026

1. What is the web portal?

The portal is the desktop companion to the Roof Inspector iPhone app. Inspectors capture photos and notes in the field; the portal lets you (or an assistant) finalise reports, edit details, generate PDFs, email customers, and manage your customer list — all from a browser on a laptop.

Inspections Customers PDF reports Email delivery Account sharing

2. Before you start

You needDetails
A modern browserChrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox on a laptop or desktop.
Your portal emailThe email the inspector invited (e.g. your work address).
Email accessYou sign in via a magic link sent to your inbox — no password.

3. Sign in for the first time

1Open the portal. Go to https://admin.roofinspector.app.
2Enter your email and click Send magic link.
3Open your inbox and click the “Sign in to Roof Inspector” link from noreply@roofinspector.app.
If you don't see it within a minute, check Junk / Spam. The link is good for 1 hour.
4You're in. The first time you sign in, your account is registered automatically.
Assistants: the inspector cannot grant you access until you have signed in at least once with your email. Sign in first, then tell the inspector you're ready to be invited.

4. How the inspector grants you access

This is a one-time step performed by the account owner (the inspector).

1Inspector signs in to admin.roofinspector.app.
2Clicks Sharing in the top navigation.
3Types the assistant's email, picks a role:
  • Editor — can view and edit inspections, customers, and photos.
  • Viewer — read-only.
4Clicks Invite. A row appears under People with access.
5The assistant signs out and back in — they will now see the inspector's data alongside their own.

5. The dashboard

After signing in you land on the inspections list. Each row shows:

  • Address — the property inspected.
  • Customer — the contact for the report.
  • Date — when the inspection took place.
  • Status — Draft, In review, or Sent.

Click any row to open the inspection.

6. Editing an inspection

1Open the inspection from the dashboard.
2Edit the address, customer, summary notes, and individual photo annotations. Each photo can have a caption, severity (None / Low / Medium / High), and a note.
3Save — changes sync back to the inspector's iPhone app automatically.
Photos are taken in the field — assistants normally don't add new photos, only refine the captions, severity, and notes.

7. Generating a PDF report

1Open the inspection.
2Click Download PDF (top right of the inspection page).
3The PDF opens in a new tab. Save it locally or email it.

The PDF is branded with the inspector's company logo, name, and contact details (set in Company Profile), and includes:

  • Cover page with property address, customer, and date.
  • Summary section with overall condition.
  • Photo grid with captions, severity badges, and notes.
  • Quote table with line items and totals (if present).

8. Emailing the report to the customer

1Open the inspection.
2Click Email Report.
3Confirm the customer's email address and edit the message if needed.
4Click Send. The customer receives the PDF as an attachment from noreply@roofinspector.app with the inspector's name in the signature.
Once sent, the inspection status moves to Sent. You can re-send if needed.

9. Customers tab

The Customers tab is your address book — name, email, phone, address. Use it to:

  • Add a new customer before creating an inspection for them.
  • Update contact details (the iPhone app sees the change immediately).
  • Search by name or email.

10. Sharing tab (account owners only)

For inspectors who want a colleague to help with reports.

  • People with access — current assistants, with their role and date granted.
  • Invite by email — type the assistant's email and pick a role.
  • Revoke — instantly removes their access. They'll see only their own data on next page load.

11. Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Magic link email never arrives Check Junk/Spam. Confirm you typed the email correctly. Try again — links expire after 1 hour.
Invite says “hasn't signed in to the portal yet” The assistant must visit admin.roofinspector.app and sign in once first. Then re-invite.
Assistant doesn't see the inspector's data Sign out and back in. Sharing takes effect on next sign-in.
PDF download fails Refresh the inspection page and try again. If it still fails, contact support.
Photos look low-resolution in the PDF Check that the iPhone app finished syncing on Wi-Fi (Settings → Sync now).

12. Security & privacy

  • Sign-in is passwordless — only people with access to your inbox can log in as you.
  • Assistants only see data for inspectors who have explicitly invited them.
  • All data is stored encrypted at rest in Supabase (EU region).
  • Photos are served via short-lived signed URLs — they cannot be shared by copying the link.
