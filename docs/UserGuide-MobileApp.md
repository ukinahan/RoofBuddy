# Roof Report — Mobile App User Guide

A simple step-by-step guide for inspectors using the Roof Report iPhone app.

---

## 1. Install & sign in

1. Install **Roof Report** from the App Store (or accept the TestFlight invite).
2. Open the app and tap **Sign in**.
3. Enter your work email address and tap **Send code**.
4. Open the email from `noreply@roofinspector.app` and tap the magic link, **or**
   type the 6-digit code into the app.
5. First-time only: complete the onboarding screens (company name, logo,
   contact details, default rate book).

> Tip: use the **same email** you'll use for the admin web portal — one login
> covers both.

---

## 2. Set up your company profile (one-time)

1. From the home screen, tap the **gear icon** (Settings).
2. Open **Company profile** and fill in:
   - Company name, phone, email, website
   - Logo — tap **Upload Logo**, pick or crop your image, and it is saved
     automatically (no need to tap **Save**). The logo is embedded directly
     into the profile, so it survives app reinstalls and appears on every
     report cover page above the customer details.
   - License number (shown on PDFs)
3. Open **Rate book** and review the default line items / prices. Edit any
   values that don't match your pricing.
4. Tap **Save** to persist the rest of the profile fields.

---

## 3. Add a customer

1. From the home screen, tap **Customers**.
2. Tap **+ New customer** (top-right).
3. Enter name, phone, email, and property address. The address is used for
   maps, directions, and the report header.
4. Tap **Save**.

You can also bulk-import customers from a CSV via **Settings → Bulk import**.

---

## 4. Start a new inspection

1. From the home screen, tap **+ New inspection**.
2. Pick the customer (or tap **+ New customer** to add one on the fly).
3. Confirm the property address and tap **Start inspection**.

---

## 5. Capture photos

1. On the inspection screen, tap the **camera button**.
2. Take photos of each area of concern. The camera auto-saves each shot to the
   inspection.
3. After each photo, you can:
   - **Annotate** — draw arrows / circles on the photo
   - **Add a note** — short caption shown under the photo on the report
   - **Set severity** — Low / Medium / High (drives the summary on the report)
   - **Tag damage type** — choose from presets (e.g. *missing shingle*,
     *cracked flashing*)
4. Tap **Done** to return to the inspection.

> Tip: hold the phone in **landscape** for wider roof shots.

---

## 6. Measure the roof (optional)

1. On the inspection screen, tap **Roof measure**.
2. Search for the property address — the satellite view will load.
3. Tap to drop points around the roof outline; tap **Close shape** to finish.
4. Enter the **pitch** (or use the **Pitch detector** to estimate from a
   photo).
5. Tap **Save** — the area, perimeter and pitch are added to the inspection.

---

## 7. Build the quote

1. On the inspection screen, scroll to **Quote** and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **+ Add line item** and pick from your rate book, or type a custom
   description.
3. Adjust quantity / unit price as needed. Totals (subtotal, tax, total) update
   automatically.
4. Tap **Save**.

---

## 8. Generate & share the PDF report

1. On the inspection screen, tap **Report** (top-right).
2. Preview the report — scroll through to confirm photos, notes and quote
   look right.
3. Tap **Share** and pick:
   - **Email** — opens Mail with the PDF attached
   - **Save to Files** — keep a local copy
   - **Print** — send to any AirPrint printer
4. To finalise, tap **Mark complete** on the inspection screen.

---

## 9. Sync with the office

- The app **auto-syncs** photos and inspection data to the cloud whenever
  you have signal (on launch, on foreground, and after each save).
- A green check on the inspection card means it has synced.
- An orange cloud icon means it's pending — connect to Wi-Fi and reopen the
  app to push it.

---

## 10. Day-to-day tips

- **Jobs tab** shows today's scheduled inspections.
- Pull down on any list to **refresh** from the cloud.
- Long-press a photo to **delete** or **reorder**.
- Settings → **Backup** lets you export everything as a single JSON file.
- If something looks off, Settings → **Send diagnostics** ships logs to
  support.

---

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Magic-link email never arrives | Check spam; tap **Resend code** after 60s |
| Photos won't upload | Settings → toggle **Sync** off and on; check Wi-Fi |
| Map won't load on Roof measure | Settings → confirm location permission is **Allow** |
| Report PDF looks blank | Force-quit the app and reopen; retry **Report** |
| Wrong company logo | Settings → Company profile → tap logo → re-upload |

Support: **support@roofinspector.app**
